01Purpose

The undersigned owner (the “Owner”) owns Palm Spring Villa, a three-bedroom villa in Canggu, Bali (the “Villa”). PT Anthony Campana Partners (“AC Partners”) markets and distributes luxury villas in Bali under its own Airbnb Superhost profile, on the main booking platforms and through its private clientele.

The Owner appoints AC Partners to market the Villa and bring it bookings. The Villa’s on-site team stays exactly the same.

02Distribution

AC Partners publishes and operates the Villa’s listings under its own accounts on Airbnb, Booking.com, Expedia, Vrbo, Marriott Homes & Villas and other platforms, on ac-collection.com, and offers the Villa to its private clientele and agent network.

03What AC Partners does

  • Publishes the listing on every platform above and on ac-collection.com.
  • Defines and manages the Villa’s pricing strategy across all channels.
  • Handles all guest communication from enquiry to arrival.
  • Provides a monthly report: bookings, occupancy, rates and market insights.

04What the Owner does

  • Keeps the Villa’s operations with its current villa manager and staff: check-in, housekeeping, maintenance and the guest experience on site.
  • Provides the house rules and check-in details.
  • Informs AC Partners within 24 hours of any change to availability or to the Villa’s condition, and blocks the Owner’s own stays in advance.

05Commission

12% for the lifetime of this agreement, on every booking generated by AC Partners. No set-up fee, no subscription, no minimum term.

The commission is calculated on the accommodation amount received after deduction of platform commission and payment fees. It excludes cleaning fees, deposits, taxes and third-party services. No commission is due on the Owner’s own direct guests.

06Payment

On Airbnb, the platform’s split payout is set at 88% to the Owner and 12% to AC Partners, paid directly by Airbnb to each Party. For bookings collected by AC Partners (other platforms, ac-collection.com, direct), the Owner’s share is transferred within 5 business days of guest check-in, with a statement for each booking. Bookings cancelled before arrival follow the cancellation policy applied at the time of booking.

07Responsibilities

The Owner is responsible for the Villa’s permits, licences, registrations and taxes as a short-term rental, and warrants that the Villa may lawfully be rented. AC Partners acts as a distribution partner only and is not liable for on-site incidents or for the condition of the premises. Each Party is responsible for its own acts and obligations.

08Term and termination

This agreement starts on the effective date and continues until terminated. Either Party may end it at any time with 30 days’ written notice, without penalty. Confirmed bookings are honoured and their commission remains due. On termination, AC Partners removes the listings and stops all distribution of the Villa.

09Confidentiality

Each Party keeps the other’s rates, commercial terms and client details confidential, during this agreement and for two years after it.

10Governing law

This agreement is governed by Indonesian law. The Parties will first seek an amicable solution; failing that, the competent courts of Badung, Bali have jurisdiction. Any change must be agreed in writing by both Parties.
