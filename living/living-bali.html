The manifesto

Every village in Bali has its own soul.

Bali is not a destination. It is an island where every village lives differently.

In Canggu, you take care of your body and get back in shape. In Ubud, you slow down, breathe and listen. In Sidemen, rice grows to the foot of the volcano and the silence is complete. In Nusa Lembongan, the day is spent in the water, barefoot. In Uluwatu, there is the cliff, the wave, and the sun falling into the sea.

Nowhere else in the world do so many ways of living, so different from one another, sit less than an hour apart.

At LIVING, we have decided to do everything it takes to turn each of them into an immersive lifestyle experience, unique in the world. Not a villa. A lifestyle villa: a place, a soul, and everything that goes with it, organised from the first morning to the last evening.

You choose a way of living. We take care of the rest.

The name

A name that says lifestyle, not influence.

LIVING

Used in this draft. Direct, international, works per village: Living Canggu, Living Ubud. The simplest to say and to film.

ART DE VIVRE

French, luxury-coded, understood everywhere. Art de Vivre Bali. Carries the idea of a way of living better than any English word.

WAYS OF LIVING

Says the concept out loud: one island, many ways to live it. Longer, but very clear on Instagram as a bio line.

SOUL

Soul Bali, Soul Canggu. Short, emotional, built on the manifesto line: every village has its soul.

To check before choosing: Instagram handle, .com domain, Indonesian and Singapore trademark.
